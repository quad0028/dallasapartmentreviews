Exactly what happens next

  1. 1

    A text from a real person — usually within minutes

    First thing we do is text you to confirm we have your request and ask anything that helps us match you better. Reply any time; it's a locator on the other end, not a bot. If a quick call would help pin down your must-haves we'll offer one — and if you'd rather keep everything to text, just reply TEXT ONLY and we will. (Late-night submits get their text in the morning.)

  2. 2

    Your request gets read, not processed

    A person goes through what you actually told us — budget, timeline, lifestyle, commute, pets, dealbreakers, and anything else you mentioned. Chasing the best of the best? Newest buildings, view units, management that residents actually praise? Say so in the text thread; that's our favorite kind of hunt.

  3. 3

    We confirm the numbers by phone

    Current quotes, the specials actually running, and how each community is screening right now — checked with leasing offices directly, not pulled from stale listings.

  4. 4

    Your compare-ready list lands — within 24–48 hours

    Side by side: pricing, specials, our honest notes from the reviews, and why each place fits you. Built so you can hold it up against anything you've been researching on your own.

And it doesn't stop at the list

Heart your favorites

We line up tours around your schedule — virtual or in person — and chase every concession, look-and-lease special, and employer discount that applies to you.

We back your application

List us as your locator when you apply. That lets us work directly with the property — verify the pricing and specials you were quoted, field qualifying questions, and help you sidestep the small mistakes that get applications declined.

Through move-in day

Approved? We'll help you line up electricity, internet, and renter's insurance so day one is plug-and-play.

Why this is free — and why we're on your side

When you sign a lease, the community pays a locator fee out of its advertising budget — and it's the same fee whichever community you pick. That means we have no reason to steer you anywhere. Our job is simple: save you the hours of cross-checking, save you money on pricing and specials, and advocate for you from first tour to signed lease. You never see a bill — and nothing on your list is ever a paid placement. Same rule as our rankings: communities can't buy their way on.
