While you wait: 3 things that speed up approval

Gather your income proof

Most recent 2–3 pay stubs, or a bank statement showing direct deposits. Self-employed? Two months of bank statements. Communities want gross monthly income at their multiple — roughly 2x at older Class C properties, 2.5x at Class B, and 3x at Class A.

Get your photo ID ready

A valid government-issued ID for each adult applying. Expired IDs slow the process. Recently moved states? Some communities accept your old license plus a piece of mail showing the new address.

Budget for move-in costs

Plan for the application and admin fees plus your deposit. Knowing your available move-in cash helps us steer you to communities whose upfront costs actually fit.
