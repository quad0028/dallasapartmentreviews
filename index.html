Common questions

How long until I hear back?
Most people get their custom list within 24–48 hours on weekdays. If you're moving soon, say so in the form — urgent timelines get prioritized.
Does it cost anything?
No. Filling out the form and getting your list costs you nothing — apartment locating is free to renters. The service is paid by the communities, never by you.
What should I include so the list actually helps?
Your budget range, the unit size you need, your ideal move-in date, and the areas of Dallas you're open to. The more honest you are about credit or rental history, the more accurate your matches.
Will I hear from more than one place?
You may be connected with one or more communities depending on what fits your budget, size, and timeline. We're matching you, not blasting your info to every leasing office in Dallas.
